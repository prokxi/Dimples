description: Golf series, events, scoring and standings for friend groups

Players register, join a series through an invite link, play events in pairings set by organisers, enter hole scores during the round and follow the standings. Golfer data is private: series data is visible only to its members, and resources a caller may not see answer 404. M1 (Series MVP) is in progress; most of its features are still Backlog tasks.
