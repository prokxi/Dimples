description: Web app where casual golfers organise and play tournament series with friends
---

Dimples lets a group of friends run their golf season: series with several events, net match play with WHS handicaps, score entry on the course and live standings. Later milestones add shot and round data from launch monitors, golf watches and tracking apps. Product vision, PRD and specs live in backlog/docs; AGENTS.md holds the architecture and coding rules.
