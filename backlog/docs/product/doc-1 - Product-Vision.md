# Product Vision

Status: Draft · Owner: prokxi · Last updated: 2026-10-11

## Vision statement

Dimples is the place where casual golfers organise their own competitions with friends and see
all their golf data in one place, with the golfer at the centre of everything.

Long term, Dimples is the single access point for everything in a golfer's game.

## Target group

**Casual golfers** who play regularly with a fixed circle of friends. They are not tour players
or club officials. They want:

- to play **their own tournaments and tournament series** without spreadsheets or group-chat
  bookkeeping, and
- to **see their golf data from several sources** (launch monitors, golf watches, round-tracking
  apps) in one place instead of in several apps.

## Problem

- Existing apps either manage *official* club competitions or track *individual* rounds.
  None of them lets a group of friends set up and run **their own tournament series** over a season.
- A golfer's data is spread across Trackman, Garmin, tracking apps and scorecards. None of them
  shows the golfer's whole game in one place.

## What makes Dimples different

- **Self-organised competition**: any golfer can create a tournament or a whole series, invite
  friends, pick a format, and get leaderboards and standings automatically.
- **Golfer-centric**: the golfer, their friends, their rounds and their data are the core
  model. Courses, clubs and providers are supporting context.
- **Source-independent data**: shots and rounds from any source are normalised into one model,
  so they can be compared and analysed together.
- **Private by default**: golfers decide what friends and tournament participants can see.

## Success for v1

Dimples v1 is a success when **our friend group can run its tournament series for the 2027
season entirely in Dimples**: set up the series, register the players, enter scores, and follow
the standings.

## Long-term direction

1. Tournaments and series among friends (v1)
2. Shot and round data from several sources in one place
3. Statistics and analysis across sources, shared with friends
4. Dimples' own round and shot tracking
5. One access point for everything in the golfer's game

## Non-goals

- **No tee-time booking** or other booking and marketplace features for courses or clubs.
- **No club administration software**: Dimples is not built for club secretaries or official
  federation competitions.
- Dimples does not replace the official handicap authority. It may calculate or display
  handicaps, but it is not the official source.
